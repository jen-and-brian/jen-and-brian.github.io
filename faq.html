Guest Information

What's the wedding dress code?

Semi-formal. Cocktail dresses, dressy jumpsuits, slacks with a blazer, or a suit are all great options.

Are plus-ones allowed?

Yes, we are allowing plus-ones as long as your wedding invitation specifically includes your plus-one.

Is the wedding kid friendly?

Yes! We will have a kids meal option of chicken tenders and fries. When you RSVP, please select the kids meal for any children in your party.

What menu options are there for people with dietary restrictions?

Dinner options include braised short ribs, grilled salmon, mushroom risotto for vegetarians, and chicken tenders and fries for children. If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please let us know when you RSVP so we can do our best to accommodate you.

Travel & Accommodations

What is the nearest airport?

The closest airport is John Wayne Airport (SNA) in Santa Ana. It is about 15 minutes from the ceremony and about 45 minutes from the reception.

Are there recommended hotels near the area?

For those coming from out of town, here are some hotels we've looked into and recommend. Keep in mind the ceremony in Fountain Valley and the reception in San Clemente are about 35 minutes apart. If you're only staying one night, we'd suggest staying near the reception so you're not driving far after a late evening.

Near the Ceremony

Avenue of the Arts — Costa Mesa. Upscale and boutique.

Crowne Plaza — Costa Mesa. Comfortable and practical, easy freeway access.

Near the Reception

San Clemente Cove — San Clemente. Condo-style suites right by the pier. Walking distance to the reception venue.

The Volare — San Clemente. Boutique hotel in downtown, walkable to shops and restaurants. Close to the reception.

Will there be a shuttle to and/or from the hotels?

We may plan to provide transportation if there is enough interest, departing from both of our parents' homes. Please indicate on your RSVP if you would be interested.
