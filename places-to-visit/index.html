Places to Visit

Turn the wedding weekend into a mini getaway. These are our favorite spots in Orange County — the places we keep coming back to.

Food

  • VOX Kitchen — Peruvian-Asian fusion; the lomo saltado is a must.
  • ROL Hand Roll Bar — The place that converted Brian into a raw seafood lover.
  • TK Burgers — Cheap, quick, and feels like a backyard BBQ.
  • Mokkoji — Our first date spot and still Jen's favorite meal.

Coffee & Drinks

  • Taty's Specialty Coffee & Tea — Go for the matcha.
  • Torotea — Boba; not a single drink has disappointed.
