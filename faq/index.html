If you have questions other than what we have listed here, please reach out directly to either Jennifer or Brian.

Event Details & Schedule

What's the wedding itinerary?

Please see our schedule page for the full itinerary.

When's the RSVP deadline?

October 15th.

Is the wedding indoors or outdoors?

The ceremony will take place indoors at a Catholic church. The cocktail hour at the reception has an open outdoor space, and dinner will be held indoors. If the weather is not cooperating, everything can be moved indoors.

Where's the wedding ceremony and reception?

The ceremony will be held at Holy Spirit Church in Fountain Valley at 12pm.

The reception will be held at La Ventura in San Clemente.

See the full schedule for more details.

Are pictures allowed during the wedding ceremony and/or reception?

Yes, we encourage photos to be taken throughout the day and night.

Guest Information

What's the wedding dress code?

Semi-formal. Cocktail dresses, dressy jumpsuits, slacks with a blazer, or a suit are all great options.

Are plus-ones allowed?

Yes, we are allowing plus-ones as long as your wedding invitation specifically includes your plus-one.

Is the wedding kid friendly?

Yes! Children are more than welcome, and we are so happy to celebrate with your little ones. We will have a kids meal option of chicken tenders and fries. When you RSVP, please select the kids meal for any children in your party. If you need a high chair, please let us know as well.

What menu options are there for people with dietary restrictions?

Dinner will include a Thai Style Salad and Bread & Butter to start, followed by your choice of entrée:

  • Miso-Glazed Salmon, Potato Purée and Broccolini with a Soy Ginger Glaze
  • Soy Braised Short Rib, Risotto and Roasted Carrots
  • Mushroom Risotto (Vegetarian)

If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please let us know when you RSVP so we can do our best to accommodate you.
