How We Met

Jennifer's Perspective

Back in December 2018, I had just finished my last semester of college and was getting ready to move to Los Angeles for a new job at Disney. With school behind me and something secure ahead, I felt ready to open my life back up and start dating again, so I downloaded Bumble.

Brian's profile stood out immediately. His bio said he needed a plus one for his work holiday party, and I remember thinking how fun that sounded. I swiped right and asked if I could be his plus one. The party had already happened by the time we met, but the first date turned out to be much better anyway.

From the moment we met, I felt calm and comfortable. It never felt like I had to perform or pretend. Being with Brian felt easy in a way I had never experienced before, and that feeling has only grown stronger ever since.

Brian's Perspective

After a streak of attending work holiday parties solo, I decided 2018 would be the year I found a date. I turned to Bumble with a very specific goal, a +1, and that's when Jennifer came along. She had to make the first move and was quickly sold on the promise of free food, drinks, and corporate small talk. Unfortunately, her timing was just late enough that I had already gone to the party alone, but that didn't stop us from planning for a future one.

I quickly got her number and suggested dinner and boba, and we settled on Mokkoji in Irvine for a Friday night. On the day of the date, I squeezed in a chest workout for a last-minute confidence boost, hoping to show up with the perfect "pump." In a very on-brand start, we ended up parking right next to each other. I texted, "I think I parked next to you," as we both sat in our cars pretending not to notice. When we finally met, I went in for a full hug while she went for the side hug. I knew the night could only get better after the half-hearted greeting.

We sat outside while waiting, chatting for about 30 minutes, during which I received an unexpected deep dive into shark facts. Only after all that did I check in with the host and learn they had been waiting for us the entire time. Honestly, it worked out, because otherwise I might never have learned that sharks don't have bones. Dinner was easy and fun, and although I tried to play it cool, we both left knowing we wanted to see each other again.

One date turned into many, and now I get to spend the rest of my life with the shark-loving girl who almost made it to that 2018 holiday party.

Since Then

After we met, Jennifer moved to Los Angeles to start her career at Disney, and Brian made the trip out to visit her whenever he could. Those visits were something to look forward to: new neighborhoods, good food, and the kind of time that makes you realize you want more of it. When the world came to a halt in 2020, we found ourselves navigating the pandemic side by side, leaning on each other through all the uncertainty, and coming out the other side more sure of us than ever.

Since then, we've made a habit of exploring the world together, collecting trips and memories along the way. Eventually those adventures led us north to Seattle, where we've taken our biggest steps yet, moving into our first apartment together and, not long after, buying our first home.
